## 📖 Project Overview

This project was developed by **Group 3** to investigate contemporary communication and technical trends through structured primary research and data analysis:

* **Survey Design & Data Collection**: Designed and distributed a structured questionnaire using digital survey forms to gather targeted demographic and behavioral response data.
* **Quantitative & Qualitative Analysis**: Cleaned and synthesized raw survey responses, categorizing themes and extracting statistically significant patterns.
* **Data Visualization & Insight Translation**: Converted survey metrics into clear visual diagrams, graphs, and narrative takeaways.
* **Technical Communication & Presentation**: Delivered a cohesive group presentation communicating key findings, user pain points, and actionable conclusions.

---

## 🛠️ Key Competencies & Methodology

* **Empirical Research**: Formulating research questions, hypothesis testing, and survey instrument design.
* **Data Processing & Analytics**: Data cleaning, response aggregation, trend analysis, and pattern identification.
* **Visual Storytelling**: Translating analytical conclusions into clear executive slides and visual graphics.
* **Collaborative Communication**: Cross-functional team coordination, peer review, and group presentation delivery.
